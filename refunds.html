REFUNDS AND CANCELLATIONS · 25 NOVEMBER 2026

Refunds and cancellations, no small print.

1718 is paid for by conversation time, in advance, with one-time payments: 1 hour, USD 5; 5 hours, USD 25. There are no subscriptions and no automatic charges. Payments are processed by Dodo Payments, which acts as Merchant of Record.

1. Canceling

There is nothing to cancel: every purchase is a one-time payment and never renews. You can stop using 1718 whenever you want.

2. Unused time

If you have not used any of a code you bought (it still has its 60 minutes), you can ask for a full refund of it within 14 days of the purchase. Once refunded, the code stops working. Time already used is not refunded, except in the case of point 3.

3. If something fails on our side

If a paid call could not be made or was cut off by an 1718 failure, we give you back the lost time or, if you prefer, its amount.

4. Charges made in error

A duplicate charge, or one you did not make, is refunded in full.

5. How to ask

Write to hola@guardianagroup.com with the date and amount of the purchase (or the receipt Dodo Payments sends you) and tell us what happened. We reply by email. The refund goes back to the same payment method; your bank may take a few days to show it.

6. Your rights

This policy does not limit the rights the law of your country gives you as a consumer, such as the right of withdrawal.
