Clear terms for using Elixire.

Elixire is a private video calling service by GUARDIANA GROUP S.A.S. (Colombian tax ID, NIT 902.119.954-6), based in Cartagena de Indias, Colombia. It runs in the browser, with no account. Until payments go live, nothing is charged.

1. What it is

Elixire lets the people in a conversation talk on video and send messages, voice notes, photos and files, end-to-end encrypted in their browsers, with a post-quantum layer on top of standard encryption. You join with an invitation link. When the conversation ends, what is left of it in the browsers is erased.

2. What it does not promise

  • No tool guarantees total security. Encryption does not protect a device or browser that is already compromised, nor stop the recipient from taking screenshots, recording or keeping copies.
  • It does not make you anonymous: the servers that connect the conversation see IP addresses (see the Privacy policy).
  • The service may change, fail or be interrupted without notice, and there is no availability guarantee.

3. Acceptable use

Use Elixire for lawful purposes. Do not use it to harass, threaten, defraud or impersonate anyone, to send illegal content, or to attack or overload the service. We may cut off anyone who abuses it, for example through large-scale automated use.

4. Your part

What you do and send with Elixire is your responsibility. Before discussing anything sensitive, compare the safety words Elixire shows you through another channel: that is what shows nobody is sitting in the middle.
