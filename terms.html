Elixire is a private video calling service by GUARDIANA GROUP S.A.S. (Colombian tax ID, NIT 902.119.954-6), based in Cartagena de Indias, Colombia. It runs in the browser, with no account. During the trial phase nothing is charged.

1. What it is

Elixire lets the people in a conversation talk on video and send messages, voice notes, photos and files, end-to-end encrypted in their browsers, with a post-quantum layer on top of standard encryption. You join with an invitation link. When the conversation ends, what is left of it in the browsers is erased.

“Check” estimates, on your own device, the probability that the other person is a real person, live.

2. What it does not promise

  • No tool guarantees total security. Encryption does not protect a device or browser that is already compromised, nor stop the recipient from taking screenshots, recording or keeping copies.
  • “Check” gives an estimate, not a certainty: it can be wrong both ways. It is not proof of identity. If someone asks for money, codes or data in a hurry, hang up and call their usual number.
  • It does not make you anonymous: the servers that connect the conversation see IP addresses (see the Privacy policy).
  • The service may change, fail or be interrupted without notice. During the trial phase there is no availability guarantee.
