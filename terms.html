Clear terms for using 1718.

1718 is a private video calling service by GUARDIANA GROUP S.A.S. (Colombian tax ID, NIT 902.119.954-6), based in Cartagena de Indias, Colombia. It runs in the browser, with no account.

1. What it is

1718 lets the people in a conversation talk on video or voice and send messages, voice notes, photos and files, end-to-end encrypted in their browsers, with a post-quantum layer on top of standard encryption. You join with an invitation link. When the conversation ends, what is left of it in the browsers is erased.

2. What it does not promise

  • No tool guarantees total security. Encryption does not protect a device or browser that is already compromised, nor stop the recipient from taking screenshots, recording or keeping copies.
  • It does not make you anonymous: the servers that connect the conversation see IP addresses (see the Privacy policy).
  • The service may change, fail or be interrupted without notice, and there is no availability guarantee.

3. Acceptable use

Use 1718 for lawful purposes. Do not use it to harass, threaten, defraud or impersonate anyone, to send illegal content, or to attack or overload the service. We may cut off anyone who abuses it, for example through large-scale automated use.

4. Your part

What you do and send with 1718 is your responsibility.

5. Price

1718 is paid for by conversation time, in advance, with one-time payments and no subscription: 1 hour, USD 5; 5 hours, USD 25. Any applicable taxes are shown before you pay.

Every day there are 2 courtesy minutes to try it on each device (each browser), with a cap per internet connection. They are used by the person who invites. Time, free or paid, runs while there are two or more people in the conversation, also outside the call, and is counted by the minute: a started minute counts in full. Paid time is always provided by the person who invites; the invited person’s time is not used in that conversation. When it runs out, the conversation pauses until the person who invited adds time.

Each hour you buy is a 60-minute code that does not expire. It is tied to the first browser that uses it and does not work in another: to continue on your phone, move the conversation with “Move to phone”. Dodo Payments also emails it to you. If you change devices or clear the browser’s data before using it up, write to us.

Payments are processed by Dodo Payments, which acts as Merchant of Record and authorized reseller of 1718; its purchase terms also apply when you pay. Nobody pays without choosing to. Refunds and cancellations: refunds.html.
