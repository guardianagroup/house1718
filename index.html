HOW THEY LISTEN IN

An ordinary call can be overheard along the way.

A phone call is only encrypted up to the cell tower. After that, it crosses networks neither of you controls.

  1. Your phone
  2. Cell tower

    Fake cell tower. It poses as your carrier’s and forces 2G, which is far weaker.

  3. Your carrier

    Inside the carrier. Lawful-interception systems can be attacked. In 2024 it emerged that they had been.

  4. Between carriers

    SS7 and Diameter. They can divert calls and texts without properly checking who asks.

  5. Their phone

    Spyware. Something like Pegasus sees everything before it is encrypted.

QUESTIONS

What people usually ask us.

Do I need to install anything or create an account?

No. It works in the browser on your phone or computer, with an invitation link. If you like, you can add 1718 to your home screen like an app.

Is anything from the conversation stored?

No. The conversation only exists on the screens of those taking part and is erased when you close it. The server relays encrypted data it cannot read.

Can anyone listen in on the call?

The call is end-to-end encrypted with hybrid post-quantum cryptography. Keys are created on your devices and the server only relays data it cannot read. One caveat: if a device is infected, whoever controls it sees what you see.

What does “post-quantum” mean?

That, on top of classical cryptography, we use ML-KEM, the standard designed so that what someone captures today stays protected against tomorrow's quantum computers.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes, in Safari and Chrome, on phones and computers.

How much does it cost?

1 hour, USD 5, or 5 hours, USD 25, with one-time payments and no subscription. Every day you get 2 free minutes to try it.

How do I pay, and how is time used?

You pay with Dodo Payments in a new tab. When you come back, each hour is a 60-minute code tied to this browser: it only works for whoever uses it first, so to continue on your phone, use “Move to phone”. Time runs by the minute while you are both in the conversation. The person who invites always pays with their own time; the invited person’s time is never used.

What happens when time runs out?

The call ends and the person who invited gets the option to buy more. As soon as they buy or paste a code, you can call again in the same conversation. If it is the other person who has time, they should be the one to invite.
