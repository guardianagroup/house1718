PRIVACY POLICY · 25 NOVEMBER 2026

No account, no cookies, no selling data.

1718 does not ask for your name, phone number or email, uses no cookies or analytics, and does not sell or share data. Even so, it needs servers so that people can find and hear each other. This is what each of them sees.

1. This website

The website is served from GitHub Pages, by GitHub, Inc. (United States). Like any web server, GitHub receives your IP address, your browser and the page you request; what it keeps is explained in its privacy statement. The pages load nothing from other companies: typefaces, images and libraries are served from this same site.

2. Cookies and storage

1718 does not use cookies. In your browser’s storage it keeps only the language you choose, your purchase codes and a random key that ties them to this browser, if you buy time, and, if needed, a technical connection preference. While you are sharing an invitation, its secret is kept in that tab and erased when you join the conversation or close it. Codes and that key are only sent to the 1718 relay, to check them and deduct minutes; nothing else is sent to anyone. To erase it all, clear the site data for house1718.com in your browser settings.

3. The conversation

Messages, voice notes, photos and files are encrypted in your browser, end to end. The GUARDIANA server that carries them (the relay, in Miami, United States) cannot read them: it sees your IP address, the room (an identifier with no name), the times and the amount of data. It keeps no names or keys, and its code writes no logs: what it needs is held in memory while the conversation lasts.

The invitation: with the link, the conversation’s secret travels in the part of the link that the browser never sends to any server. If a code is used instead, the secret waits on the relay, locked with a key derived from that code, for at most 10 minutes and for a single use.

When the conversation ends, what was left of it in the browsers is erased: messages, files and keys.

Time: to count each conversation’s time, the relay checks purchase codes with Dodo Payments and stores, for each code, only an irreversible digest (SHA-256), the minutes used and the digest of the key of the browser that used it first; it does not know who bought it. The 2 courtesy minutes are counted per device, with the digest of its browser key, and with a cap per IP address: the relay keeps them only in memory, with the address mixed with a key that changes every time it starts, and forgets them after 24 hours.

4. Calls and video calls

They always go through GUARDIANA’s call server in Miami (United States). It sees the IP addresses of the people talking, the times and the volume, not the picture or the sound, and is configured without logs. Picture and sound are end-to-end encrypted and also carry a post-quantum layer that only the browsers in the conversation can open. The camera and microphone are only used during the call and with your permission.

In a group call, each person joins through whoever added them: that person’s device receives your picture, your voice and your messages as in a normal call and passes them on to the others, encrypted again for each of them. The servers still cannot see the picture or hear the sound.

5. Changing your voice or hiding your face

During the video call you can change your voice or hide your face (it becomes a mosaic). This happens on your device, before encryption, and the other person sees a notice that it is on.

6. What the other person receives

What you send and, during the call, your picture and voice. In a group, the same reaches each person in the call.

7. What we do not do

We do not sell or share data. There is no advertising, no analytics, no accounts and no profiles of the people who use 1718. Payments are processed by Dodo Payments: your card details go to Dodo Payments, not to us. Dodo Payments only tells us what we need to give you the time you bought and to handle refunds (the amount, the date and the email you enter when paying), and its privacy policy also applies to that payment.

8. If you write to us

If you write to hola@guardianagroup.com, we keep that email to answer you and for nothing else. It is deleted one year after the last reply, or earlier if you ask.

9. Your rights, legal basis and complaints

You can ask us what data of yours we hold, to correct it or to delete it, by writing to hola@guardianagroup.com. Almost everything on this page lives only in your browser or in the memory of a conversation that has already ended, so it does not exist anywhere we could look for it. We process connection data because it is needed to provide the service you ask for, and your email, if you write to us, with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. 1718’s servers are in the United States, as is GitHub, which serves the website. If you think we handle your data wrongly, you can complain to the Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio (Colombia), the AEPD (Spain) or the data protection authority of your country; first, write to us and we will sort it out.
